Who We Are

We are a dedicated group of students committed to raising awareness about human rights, gender equality, and the prevention of violence against women and girls. Through this digital campaign, our goal is to open a respectful space to discuss how women's rights have been historically overlooked and why they must be unconditionally protected today.

"Women's rights are human rights. Raising our voices today builds a safer tomorrow for everyone."

Our team believes that education, empathy, and active participation are the core tools to dismantle gender-based violence. By combining historical analysis, creative media, and clear communication, we aim to inspire our school community to take action and promote safe, respectful relationships every single day.
